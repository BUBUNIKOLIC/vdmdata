Coach,

I'm an assistant with the Italian women's national team. At the World Cup in Berlin I was the one preparing the opponents — USA, China, Czechia, Australia. Rosters, lineups, play types, shooting charts, the lot.

Doing that by hand takes an evening per team, so over the years I wrote my own tool for it. One thing it does that I haven't seen elsewhere: you draw a play and watch it run in 3D, in the right order — first the screen, then the player using it, then the pass.

2D — for the board
3D — for the players
TOP PICK — a ball screen on the move: the ball is reversed first, so the help is a step late and the roll goes into an empty lane. Watch it run, 15s

The players watch that on their phone instead of reading a paper sheet. It's yours to use however you like — team meeting, your clinic, your social. Nothing to credit, nothing to sign up for.

And that’s one of five things the program does. The others are the ones that eat your week: tagging the game film, cutting reels by player, the scouting report on the opponent, and the practice plan. Same program, same teams, one place — which is why I wrote it in the first place.

If preparing opponents is part of your week too, I'm happy to show you how I do it. No sales pitch: I'm a coach, not a salesman.
